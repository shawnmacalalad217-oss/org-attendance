About

A website for student organizations to record event attendance with QR codes. Each student has one account and one personal QR code that works for every organization they join, so there are no paper attendance sheets and no separate sign-ups per event.

Features

One account, one QR

Sign up once. Your personal QR code works in every org you join.

Many organizations

Students can request a new org. The admin approves requests and limits how many orgs exist.

QR attendance

Officers scan QR codes with a phone or laptop camera, or type a student number.

Yearly membership

Memberships last 1 year. Members re-register when they expire.

Officer roles

President, Vice President, Secretary and Treasurer, with presidency transfer.

Attendance history

Members see the events they attended; officers export lists as CSV.

Roles

RoleWhat they can do
MemberJoin orgs, show QR code, see own attendance, re-register.
Secretary / TreasurerEverything a member can, plus create events, scan attendance and view member lists.
Vice PresidentOfficer tools, plus assign Secretary/Treasurer, remove members and edit org details.
PresidentEverything, including appointing a Vice President and transferring the presidency.
AdminApproves or rejects org requests, sets the org limit and can delete orgs.

How it works

  1. Create an account with your name, student number and course & year.
  2. Register as a member of any organization.
  3. At an event, show your QR code. An officer scans it and your time-in is saved.
  4. Check your attended events anytime on the organization's page.

The project

. Built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) for accounts and data, hosted on GitHub Pages.
